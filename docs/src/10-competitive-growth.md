## Proposed differentiation (pending client approval — not confirmed positioning)

Experience/longevity, professional presentation, event-scale capability, brand trust from the wider GreenChillyz name, menu variety, stronger digital experience than competitors currently show.

## Growth opportunities (marked as growth ideas, not V1 website requirements)

Google Ads on catering-intent keywords; WedMeGood/WeddingWire vendor profiles; premium menu tiers; cross-selling with decor/photography/DJ vendors; email nurture sequences; corporate repeat contracts; referral program; WhatsApp automation; CRM adoption; structured review generation; blog/content marketing.

Topics: wedding catering tips (Bhubaneswar-specific), outdoor event menu ideas, guest-count planning, buffet planning, live-station ideas.
