## 12. Executive Summary

### Top 10 critical issues

1. No SSL/HTTPS security certificate
2. Missing online inquiry form (phone/email only)
3. Very weak SEO (meta titles, keywords, heading structure)
4. Outdated imagery (2022 SlideServe content)
5. No pricing transparency on website
6. Google Business profiles unoptimised for 4 locations
7. No WedMeGood / WeddingWire vendor profile
8. Restaurant ratings (3.4/5) conflated with catering trust
9. No WhatsApp Business integration
10. Missing privacy policy and compliance pages

### Top 10 quick wins (implement within 7 days)

1. Install SSL certificate (free via hosting provider)
2. Add WhatsApp floating button (+91-9090022220)
3. Create 4-field inquiry form on homepage
4. Optimise homepage meta title: "Best Wedding Catering Bhubaneswar | Green Chillyz 15+ Years"
5. Add pricing packages section (₹300/450/600/800 per plate — pending client confirmation)
6. Create WedMeGood vendor profile with 10+ event photos
7. Add 5 catering testimonials (separate from restaurant ratings)
8. Upload FSSAI licence + "500+ Events Completed" badge (after client verification)
9. Add "Get Free Quote" CTA in header
10. Create/optimise Google Business profiles for all 4 locations
