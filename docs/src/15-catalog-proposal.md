## Why a premium catalog?

A premium catalog:

- **Builds trust before discussing price** — positions Greenchillyz as a luxury catering brand, not a commodity vendor
- **Justifies premium pricing** — reduces negotiation pressure by demonstrating quality
- **Reduces buyer friction** — helps clients visualise the complete event experience before an in-person meeting
- **Works as a multi-channel sales tool** — shareable via WhatsApp, email, at exhibitions, and in-person meetings
- **Showcases depth** — chefs, hygiene standards, buffet styling, and live food stations that a website cannot fully convey in one scroll

> **Sales message:** Don't sell dishes. Sell unforgettable experiences. Every page should reinforce Greenchillyz's professionalism, luxury presentation, culinary expertise, and hospitality.

## Production notes

- **Photography:** Real event photography is the single most important asset for both catalog and website. A professional shoot covering weddings, corporate setups, live stations, and buffet presentations is a prerequisite before either can be produced at the intended quality level.
- **Copy approval:** All claims (founding year, event count, FSSAI, awards) must be verified by the client before inclusion in the catalog or the website. See [Scope & Conflicts](11-scope-and-conflicts.md).
- **Pricing:** If the catalog is to include pricing, confirm the pricing structure with the client. The recommended approach is package-based tiers rather than per-dish pricing.
- **Format:** The catalog should be produced as a high-resolution PDF (for print and WhatsApp sharing) and optionally as an interactive digital version (for email and exhibitions).
- **Update cycle:** Commit to an annual refresh of gallery, testimonials, and awards sections to keep the catalog current.
