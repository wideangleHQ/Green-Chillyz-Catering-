# Users, Personas & Journeys

## Personas

| Persona                     | Needs                                                             | Key questions                                       | Content that converts                             | Trigger                                |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Wedding Client              | Large capacity, menu customization, live counters, top-tier trust | "Can they handle 500 guests? Do they look premium?" | Wedding gallery, signature menus, testimonials    | Get a Quote after gallery+testimonials |
| Corporate Client            | Reliability, professionalism, repeatable service                  | "Will this be consistent and on-time?"              | Corporate catering page, service reliability copy | Simple form, fast response promise     |
| Birthday/Private Client     | Flexible smaller packages, attractive presentation                | "What can I get for my budget/guest count?"         | Package tiers, casual event gallery               | Low-friction enquiry                   |
| Event Planner/Coordinator   | Vendor reliability, menus, capacity, fast turnaround              | "Can I trust them with my client's event?"          | Full menu PDF, capacity info, gallery             | Direct contact / quick quote           |
| Returning/Referral Customer | Speed, familiarity                                                | "How do I reach them again?"                        | Prominent phone/WhatsApp                          | One-tap contact                        |

## User journeys

| #   | Journey             | Entry                 | Key pages                                                        | CTA                | Conversion event    | Drop-off risk                         |
| --- | ------------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ | ------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Wedding enquiry     | Google/referral       | Home → Wedding Catering → Gallery → Menus → Testimonials → Quote | Get a Quote        | Form submit         | Vague hero, no wedding-specific proof |
| 2   | Corporate enquiry   | Google/LinkedIn       | Home → Corporate Catering → Contact                              | Get a Quote / Call | Form/call           | No B2B-specific copy                  |
| 3   | Private celebration | Google/social         | Home → Birthday/Celebrations → Packages                          | Get a Quote        | Form submit         | No visible pricing anchor             |
| 4   | Menu browsing only  | Any                   | Menus → Regional/International Cuisine                           | Explore/Download   | Menu view event     | No path back to quote                 |
| 5   | Past-event research | Google Images/social  | Gallery                                                          | View more / Quote  | Gallery→Quote click | Gallery buried in nav                 |
| 6   | Immediate WhatsApp  | Any page              | Floating WhatsApp button                                         | Chat on WhatsApp   | whatsapp_click      | Button not visible on mobile fold     |
| 7   | Phone call          | Any page, esp. mobile | Header/Contact                                                   | Call now           | phone_click         | Number not tappable                   |

Each journey should reach a CTA within 2–3 clicks of entry.
